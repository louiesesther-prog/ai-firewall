## Chrome Web Store

**Short description (132 chars max):**
> Auto-detect and mask 30+ PII types in AI chats. 100% local, Luhn-validated CC, confidence scoring. Protects ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot & more.

**Detailed description:**

> # 🛡️ AI Personal Firewall
>
> **Keep your sensitive data private when using AI chat applications.**
>
> AI Personal Firewall automatically detects and masks Personally Identifiable Information (PII) in your prompts before they reach AI providers. Processing is 100% local — your data never leaves your browser.
>
> ## ✨ Features
>
> * **30+ PII Types Detected** — Email, phone, SSN, credit cards, IP addresses, crypto wallets, passwords, API keys, MAC addresses, DOB, passport numbers, driver licenses, street addresses, bank accounts, routing numbers, JWT tokens, AWS keys, GitHub tokens, Slack tokens, and more
> * **10 International PII Types** — UK National Insurance/NHS, India Aadhaar/PAN, China 18-digit ID, Canada SIN, Australia TFN, Japan My Number, Brazil CPF/CNPJ, France INSEE
> * **Luhn Checksum Validation** — Distinguishes real credit card numbers from random digits (valid cards get 0.95 confidence, invalid drop to 0.3)
> * **Confidence Scoring** — Every detection includes a 0.0–1.0 confidence score based on context, patterns, and validation
> * **Smart Context Heuristics** — Reduces false positives by analyzing surrounding text (e.g., version numbers, math expressions, product codes)
> * **Realistic Fake-Data Masking** — Option to replace PII with realistic fake data instead of placeholders
> * **Automatic Restore** — AI responses have placeholders restored to original values transparently
> * **Privacy Dashboard** — View scrubbing stats, PII type breakdown, and detection history
> * **Toggle Protection** — Enable/disable with one click
> * **17 Supported AI Platforms** — ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Poe, Groq, Mistral, Cohere, Meta AI, DeepSeek, You.com, Kimi, Qwen, Jina, Phind, Google AI Chat
>
> ## 🔒 Privacy First
>
> * **100% Local Processing** — All PII detection and masking happens in your browser. Zero data sent to external servers.
> * **No Logging** — PII values exist only in memory and are cleared on page refresh.
> * **Open Source** — MIT licensed. Full source available on GitHub.
>
> ## 🎯 How It Works
>
> 1. **Type** your prompt into any supported AI chat
> 2. **Auto-Detect** — The extension scans text for 30+ PII patterns with Luhn validation and context heuristics
> 3. **Auto-Mask** — Detected PII is replaced with placeholders or realistic fake data
> 4. **Send Safely** — The scrubbed prompt is sent to the AI — your real data stays in your browser
> 5. **Auto-Restore** — When the AI responds, original values are restored automatically
>
> ## 📊 Privacy Dashboard
>
> Click the shield icon in your toolbar to open the dashboard. View real-time stats including requests scrubbed, PII items masked, per-type breakdown, and toggle protection on/off.
